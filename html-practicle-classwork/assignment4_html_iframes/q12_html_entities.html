HTML Special Entities Showcase

In HTML, reserved characters must be replaced with entities to avoid parser errors:

  • Less than symbol: < (Entity: &lt;)
  • Greater than symbol: > (Entity: &gt;)
  • Ampersand symbol: & (Entity: &amp;)
  • Double quotation mark: " (Entity: &quot;)
  • Single quotation mark: ' (Entity: &apos;)

Copyright and Registered Trademarks

Copyright symbol: © (Entity: &copy;)
Trademark symbol: ™ (Entity: &trade;)
Registered Trademark symbol: ® (Entity: &reg;)

Sample Statement: 5 < 10 & 20 > 15 "TechCorp"™ © 2026 All Rights Registered®.
